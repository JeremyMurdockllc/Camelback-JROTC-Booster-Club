Camelback High School Booster Club Startup Kit
Phoenix Union High School District

How to use
1. Open fill.html (or index.html) in a web browser. Fill club name, school, addresses, and people. Documents auto-fill. Download roster, bylaws, articles amendment, and banking resolution as .doc / .txt / CSV.
2. Read Camelback_Booster_Club_Startup_Playbook.pdf for the longer step packet.
3. Word templates in this folder are optional backups if someone prefers Word over the website.

Do not fundraise until the principal / PXU has approved the club.

Not legal, tax, or district advice. Confirm filings with PXU, the Arizona Corporation Commission, and the IRS.
