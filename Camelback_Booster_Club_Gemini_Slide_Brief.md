## Slide 2 — What a booster is (PXU definition)

- Any parent or community group that gives **value** to a school program, student activity, or student organization
- Athletic or non-athletic
- Purpose: **assist and support**, never **supplant** the school program
- Superintendent owns the educational program
- Principal owns campus activities and fundraising
- Certified staff sponsor = liaison only

*Notes: Quote KJA-OP-1. “Approval” always means prior written approval of the principal or designee.*

## Slide 4 — Do this in this order

1. Principal / AD permission — **no fundraising yet**
2. Recruit four parent officers
3. Adopt constitution and bylaws
4. File Arizona **Articles of Incorporation** (Form C011) — not an LLC
5. Get a free EIN from IRS.gov
6. Open a two-signature bank account
7. File **Articles of Amendment** with IRS 501(c)(3) language
8. File Form 1023-EZ on Pay.gov
9. Submit two signed PXU applications to the principal
10. Re-apply every year. Year-end due **August 31**

## Slide 7 — Roles process flow (money)

Need / request  
→ President checks mission + principal approval  
→ 3–4 board members vote yes  
→ Treasurer pays (two signatures + receipt)  
→ Secretary files it with the minutes  
→ Treasurer reconciles the bank within 30 days  

Nobody spends club money alone. Not the coach. Not the Treasurer acting solo.

## Slide 8 — Constitution and bylaws (PXU requires both)

**Constitution must name**
- Name and purpose
- Who can be a member
- Officers, duties, term limits
- How to amend (board 2/3, or written petition of 66% of members, then ballot)
- How to adopt / ratify

**Bylaws must name**
- Board powers and committees
- Succession and elections
- Fiscal year and internal controls
- Who approves spending **before** any commitment
- Meeting schedule, quorum, who runs the meeting

Do **not** file bylaws with the Arizona Corporation Commission. Vote them in. Keep a signed copy for PXU and the IRS.

## Slide 9 — Arizona corporation (two-step)

**Not an LLC. Not Articles of Organization.**

**Step 1 — Form C011** (ACC default language)  
- Arizona Business Center: arizonabusinesscenter.azcc.gov  
- Cover sheet + C011 + C003 disclosure + statutory-agent acceptance  
- **$40**  
- Phoenix / Maricopa: **no newspaper ad** (ACC database posting)

**Step 2 — Articles of Amendment**  
- Add IRS purpose, no-inurement, no politics, dissolution  
- About **$25**  
- Do this **before** Form 1023-EZ

## Slide 10 — EIN and bank

**EIN**
- Free at IRS.gov after the corporation exists
- Anyone charging for an EIN is a scam
- Never use the school or PUHSD EIN

**Bank**
- Account title = corporate legal name
- Two signatures on every check (President + Treasurer)
- Take: stamped articles, EIN letter, banking resolution, photo IDs
- Treasurer is the person authorized to open the account

---

## Slide 11 — Federal 501(c)(3)

- Arizona nonprofit ≠ tax-exempt donations
- File **1023-EZ** on Pay.gov after the amendment is approved
- Typical new booster: receipts ≤ $50,000, assets ≤ $250,000
- User fee **$275** (full Form 1023 is $600)
- File within **27 months** of incorporation for retroactive exemption
- Every year: Form **990-N** if receipts normally ≤ $50,000  
  (old PXU handout still says $25,000 — that figure is outdated)

No tax-deductible receipts until the IRS determination letter arrives.

## Slide 14 — Money rules (do not break these)

- No individual student accounts
- Students pay trip fees to the **campus bookstore**
- Boosters do **not** sign hotel / bus / airfare contracts
- Cash donations above the application cap need extra Governing Board approval
- Equipment donations **always** need Board approval
- No petty cash for a coach
- No raffles on PXU property (AG Opinion I84-018)
- If cadets/students work a fundraiser, split net proceeds between the booster club and the school JROTC activity account (bookstore). Write the % on the fundraiser form (AG Opinion I84-032). No cash to individual students.
- Two people count cash
- No school letterhead
- PXU is not liable for booster contracts

---

## Slide 15 — Staff, students, fundraising

- Parents plan fundraisers — not students
- No sales quotas
- Door-to-door is voluntary only
- Student-organization fundraisers take precedence
- Concessions need principal approval + Maricopa County food rules
- Facility use needs a Facility Rental Agreement; some uses need **$1,000,000** COI naming PXU
- PXU Trust insurance covers an **approved** booster only if a school administrator co-sponsors the activity
- Do not give awards directly to a student — donate to the school
- Do not hire or pay coaches / clinicians
- Gifts to a coach need prior written principal approval
- If the District asks for records and the club refuses, approval is revoked
